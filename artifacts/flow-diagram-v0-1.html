0. System map

Who talks to whom, and what is stored where. Nothing else exists in v1.

Refs: D4 D8 D12 D23 D30 R9 R10

  • Acts via app screens and alerts

  • Disclosure + OAuth handoff
  • List, evidence, unknowns card, bottom sheet

Points for sign-off

Places where drawing the flow forced a choice the PRD didn't make. Each is my proposal; accept, change, or debate.

  1. Alert send time. Steps fire at 09:00 in the user's local time rather than at the exact minute the date was set. Reason: a 03:00 push is noise; a morning one is actionable.
  2. Active subscription, date passes, no receipt seen (7C). Proposed: stay active, roll the estimated date forward one period, and flag "no receipt seen for last date" on the record. Alternative: move it to likely_cancelled. Rolling forward errs toward keeping a warning alive; moving to likely_cancelled errs toward a quieter list. The PRD only defined this branch for cancellation_pending.
  3. Charged while cancellation_pending (7C). Proposed: return to active and notify "charged although you marked it cancelled", but do not count it in M1, because an alert was delivered. Alternative: count it anyway as a stricter definition of surprise.
  4. Disconnecting one account when a record was merged from two. Proposed: delete only that account's evidence and keep the record if the other account still has evidence for it. Alternative: delete the whole record.
  5. Receipt lookback for "no receipt seen" (7C). Proposed: check at T+3 days, to tolerate late receipts. Alternative: check on T+0 and risk false "likely cancelled".
  6. Attachments (3). Proposed: extract the first page of a PDF only when the body has no amount at all. Keeps LLM input small; may miss amounts buried on page 2.
  7. Re-subscribe question left unanswered (7). Proposed: tracking resumes, the question stays on the record, and it is not counted in M1 until answered.
  8. Partial OAuth grant (1). Treated as no grant. Google allows users to untick scopes; with only one scope requested this should be rare, but the branch exists.
